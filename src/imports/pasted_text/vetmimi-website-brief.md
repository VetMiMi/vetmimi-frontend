CORE CREATIVE DIRECTION
Name the visual idea "Structured Expression".
The clean interface is the structure. The abstract art language is the expression.
The site should feel like an editorial art gallery + a quiet reflective space + a modern professional wellbeing practice.
Do not make it look like a clinic, hospital, SaaS dashboard, beige therapist template, spiritual wellness template or children's art-therapy site.

THE SITE ITSELF SHOULD FEEL LIKE ART.
Use large 2D abstract color fields, flowing waves, translucent ribbons, imperfect halos, petal forms, nested organic shapes, hand-drawn lines, controlled collage fragments and strong negative space.
Avoid glossy 3D blobs, glassmorphism, neon gradients, generic tech wallpaper and random decorative circles.
Most sections should still have flat boundaries. Approximate section-divider frequency: 60% flat, 25% gentle wave, 10% double wave, 5% dramatic curve.
Maximum about one major abstract gesture plus one minor accent per viewport.

TYPOGRAPHY
Use Fraunces or a similar expressive editorial serif for large emotional headings, H1s and pull quotes.
Use Manrope or a similar modern highly-readable sans serif for body copy, navigation, forms, buttons and practical information.
Home Hero H1: around 80-92px desktop, 46-54px mobile.
Page H1: around 60-72px desktop, 42-50px mobile.
Body: 17-18px.
Do not center everything. Use controlled asymmetry.

GRID / SPACING
Desktop reference: 1440px, 12-column grid, max content width around 1240px.
Tablet 768px, mobile 390px, but make the implementation fluid.
Use an 8px spacing system.
Desktop section spacing 120-160px. Mobile 72-96px.
Long-form reading width around 680-760px.
Functional card radius 16-20px. Creative feature cards can have one exaggerated 40-48px corner.

GLOBAL NAVIGATION
Desktop header: VetMiMi Logo | About | Services | Art of Wellness | Portfolio | Stories & Insights | Contact | Book Appointment.
Logo returns Home; do not add Home as a separate desktop nav item.
Book Appointment is the only filled nav CTA and uses Poppy Coral.
Header can start transparent/cream and become warm cream with a subtle border after scroll.
Mobile preferred layout: Logo | compact Book | Menu.

BUTTONS
Primary: Poppy Coral fill, accessible text contrast.
Secondary: transparent/white with Deep Indigo border.
Editorial links: text + simple organic arrow.
Do not make buttons into amorphous blobs.

FOOTER
Deep Indigo background, cream text, irregular wave top edge. Small coral petal outline in one corner and a tiny indigo/cream vessel pattern in another. Columns: Brand, Explore, Connect, Information. Keep decorative patterns away from link text.

CONTENT VOICE
Use simple English. Warm, human and clear.
Prefer: "Sometimes words are not the only way in."
Prefer: "You do not need to know how to draw."
Prefer: "Tell us a little about your idea."
Avoid corporate phrases such as "holistic evidence-based interventions designed to facilitate wellbeing".
Avoid "healing journey", "transform your life", guaranteed outcomes, crisis-service implications or unsupported clinical promises.
Anything marked PROVISIONAL / VERIFY / TO CONFIRM must remain a placeholder. Do not invent qualifications, fees, dates, age ranges, locations, languages, registrations, Art of Wellness roles or outcomes.

RESPONSIVE ART RULE
Desktop may use halo + ribbon + art crop. Mobile should normally keep only one of those major gestures. Do not squeeze every desktop decoration onto a phone.
Mobile priority: title -> explanation -> primary action -> supporting image/art.

ACCESSIBILITY
WCAG AA contrast. Visible focus. Keyboard-accessible filters/forms/calendar. Touch targets around 44px. Do not rely on color alone for status, selected dates, availability or errors. Decorative SVGs are aria-hidden. Meaningful images need alt text. Do not put body text over busy artwork.
